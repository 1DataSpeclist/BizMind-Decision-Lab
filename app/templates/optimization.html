How Recommendations Are Calculated

1. Average Daily Demand: Units sold during the last 30 days divided by 30.

2. Target Stock: The greater of minimum stock and 14 days of estimated demand.

3. Purchase Quantity: The quantity required to reach target stock, rounded up to a multiple of the supplier's MOQ.

4. Status: Products at or below minimum stock are marked Urgent Reorder.

Recommendations are estimates based on synthetic sales data. The 14-day coverage period is an initial planning assumption, not a verified supplier lead time.
