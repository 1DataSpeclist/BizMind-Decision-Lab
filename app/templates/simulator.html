How the Simulator Works

1. Baseline: Uses the product's current selling price, cost price and sales from the last 30 days.

2. Demand Adjustment: Applies the manually entered percentage change to historical demand.

3. Stock Constraint: Projected sales cannot exceed available inventory.

4. Revenue: Calculated by multiplying projected units sold by the selling price.

5. Profit: Calculated by subtracting total product cost from estimated revenue.

6. Business Impact: Compares scenario profit with baseline profit and calculates remaining stock and unfulfilled demand.

Results are hypothetical estimates based on synthetic historical sales data. Demand changes are manually assumed and are not automatically predicted from price changes. These estimates do not guarantee future outcomes.
